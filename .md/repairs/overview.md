# Repairs overview

The full flow from intake to invoice. Nothing moves by itself: a person does every step on the repair board.

**Where:** Repairs › Repair board. A point of sale of the kind Repair shop opens the same board. **Who:** the screen `repairs`, and the document types given to you or your role.

## The flow

| Step | Done by hand | Document | Stock and balances |
|------|--------------|----------|--------------------|
| 1. New job | Press New job | Repair intake, books nothing | None |
| 2. Fill the intake | Enter the properties in the document | Same | None |
| 3. Offer | Press Make the offer, write parts and repair time | Repair offer, books nothing; posted before the invoice | None |
| 4. Decide | Press Accepted or Refused | The offer records who and when | None |
| 5. Bill | Press Make the bill when the client comes back | Draft sales invoice | None yet |
| 6. Post the invoice | Post it in the document screen | Sales invoice, services | Parts leave stock, the client owes the amount |

Stages on the board are separate: a person moves the job through them. No step above moves it.

Payment is the normal sales flow, not this board.
